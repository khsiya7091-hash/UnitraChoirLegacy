Our Story

Honouring the past. Celebrating the journey.

The UNITRA Choir was established in 1987 at the University of Transkei. More than a choir, it became a family united by music, friendship, and purpose across generations.

Mission

To ensure the enduring legacy of the UNITRA Choir.

Vision

To inspire future generations through choral excellence.
